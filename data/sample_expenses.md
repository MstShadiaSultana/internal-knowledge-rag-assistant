# Fictional Example: Expense Reimbursement

Employees submit expense claims through the finance portal within 14 calendar days of a business purchase. A receipt is required for purchases above EUR 20. The direct manager approves the claim before the finance team reviews it. Personal purchases are not reimbursable. For a missing receipt, write a short explanation in the claim and ask the finance team for guidance. Payments are normally included in the next monthly reimbursement cycle after approval.
